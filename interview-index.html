M1 · The Math You Actually Need (22 questions)

Vectors Are Meaning

  • What does it mean for two embeddings to be "close", and why is that useful?
  • Why are embeddings hundreds of dimensions instead of 2 or 3?
  • Can you interpret an individual dimension of an embedding?
  • What does king − man + woman ≈ queen actually demonstrate?
  • You embed 1M documents with model A, then upgrade to model B. Can you compare A-vectors to B-queries?

Dot Product = Similarity

  • Explain cosine similarity to a non-ML engineer in two sentences.
  • When do dot product and cosine similarity give the same ranking? Why does it matter?
  • Why might raw dot product be a bad relevance score for unnormalized embeddings?
  • Is a cosine similarity of 0.75 "good"?
  • What's the computational cost of cosine similarity between two d-dimensional vectors, and of searching n documents?
  • Two sentences with no words in common — can they still score high cosine similarity?

Matrices Transform Meaning

  • Answers
  • What is a parameter in a neural network, concretely?
  • Why is matrix multiplication the dominant operation in deep learning?
  • A layer maps 384-D vectors to 1536-D. What's its weight matrix shape and parameter count?
  • Why can't two matrices multiply if the inner dimensions differ?
  • Is a stack of two matrix multiplications more expressive than one?

Slopes, Gradients & Learning

  • Explain gradient descent without math.
  • What happens with a learning rate that's too high? Too low? How would you detect each from the loss curve?
  • What exactly is the gradient of the loss, dimensionally?
  • Does gradient descent find the best possible parameters?
  • State the chain rule intuitively and say why it matters for deep learning.

M2 · Word Embeddings, For Real This Time (21 questions)

One-Hot & Why It Fails

  • What's the difference between one-hot encoding and an embedding?
  • Why is the orthogonality of one-hot vectors a problem for NLP?
  • State the distributional hypothesis and explain why it matters practically.
  • Is TF-IDF completely obsolete then?
  • Word embeddings are ~300-D, one-hots are ~50,000-D. Why does shrinking dimensions add information?

word2vec: Skip-Gram Internals

  • Explain skip-gram's training objective in one paragraph.
  • Why does word2vec use two embedding matrices instead of one?
  • What does self-supervised mean, and why was word2vec a landmark example?
  • Skip-gram vs CBOW — differences and when to prefer each?
  • How does the window size hyperparameter change what's learned?
  • Where do word2vec-style embedding tables appear inside modern transformers?

Negative Sampling

  • Why is the full softmax over a vocabulary too expensive for word2vec, and what does negative sampling change?
  • Why are negatives sampled with frequency^0.75 rather than uniformly or by frequency?
  • What is contrastive learning, and name systems beyond word2vec that use it.
  • What happens if your "negative" samples are secretly positives?
  • Sketch how you'd apply negative sampling to train a retrieval model for RAG.

Properties & Limits of Embeddings

  • Static vs contextual embeddings — the canonical question. Answer it well.
  • Compare word2vec, GloVe and fastText in three sentences.
  • How can word embeddings be biased, and what would you do about it in production?
  • Why can't more training data fix the polysemy problem for static embeddings?
  • Your vector search behaves badly on short, ambiguous queries. Explain why, using this module's concepts.

Softmax & Cross-Entropy

The Training Loop

  • Batch size: what actually changes when you raise it from 8 to 512?

Backpropagation, Intuitively

  • Explain backpropagation without heavy notation.
  • What causes vanishing gradients, and name four mitigations.
  • Why does training take ~3× the compute of inference, and much more memory?
  • What do loss.backward(), optimizer.step() and optimizer.zero_grad() each do?
  • Why do gradients "add where paths merge"?

Optimizers & Regularization

  • Why do transformer schedules use learning-rate warmup?
  • How does weight decay reduce overfitting?
  • Dropout: how does it work at train vs inference time, and why does it help?
  • Your loss spikes to NaN mid-training. Give a diagnostic checklist.

Measuring Models: Precision, Recall, F1

  • When is accuracy a misleading metric, and what do you use instead?
  • Define precision and recall as plain questions, and give a case favoring each.
  • Why is F1 the harmonic rather than arithmetic mean?
  • Micro vs macro F1 — difference, and when each misleads.
  • How do you choose a classification threshold in production?
  • Your new transformer classifier hits 91% accuracy. The interviewer asks: "Good?" What do you need to answer?

Build: A Tiny Classifier

  • Why did the 2-layer network barely beat logistic regression on TF-IDF features?
  • Using a frozen embedding model + light classifier vs fine-tuning the full model — trade-offs?
  • Why must dropout behave differently at train vs eval time, and where did that appear in the code?

Language Modeling & Perplexity

  • Is an LLM "just autocomplete"? Give the sophisticated version of both sides.

RNNs & LSTMs, Briefly

  • Why did transformers replace RNNs/LSTMs? (the canonical question)
  • What problem do LSTM gates solve, and what do they NOT solve?
  • Are RNNs completely dead in 2020s practice?

Seq2Seq & the Bottleneck

  • What is the seq2seq bottleneck, precisely?
  • Where do the terms "encoder" and "decoder" come from and why do they persist?
  • Why does the final RNN state over-represent the end of the input?
  • Design question: without knowing about attention, how would you fix the bottleneck?

Attention Is Born

  • Explain attention (the 2015 kind) in three sentences.
  • Why must attention be "soft" (a weighted average) rather than picking the best state?
  • Define query, key and value in one line each, with the retrieval analogy.
  • How did attention solve BOTH of the RNN's structural problems?
  • What's the computational cost of attention over a sequence of length n, and why will it matter later?

M5 · The Transformer, Piece by Piece (41 questions)

Tokenization: BPE & WordPiece

  • What are the trade-offs in choosing vocabulary size?

Embeddings & Positional Encoding

  • Why do transformers need positional encoding when RNNs didn't?
  • Compare sinusoidal, learned, and rotary (RoPE) positional encodings.
  • Why does RoPE make attention depend on relative rather than absolute position?
  • The embedding table is over half of MiniLM's parameters. Is that typical? What changes at LLM scale?
  • Can you run a 512-position model on a 2,000-token document? What actually happens?

Self-Attention: Q, K, V

  • Walk through self-attention end to end. (THE interview question — have this cold.)
  • Why three separate projections instead of using the embedding directly for all roles?
  • Why divide by √d_k?
  • What's the complexity of self-attention and what are the practical consequences?
  • How does self-attention solve the problem static word embeddings couldn't?
  • Does a token attend to itself? Is that necessary?

Multi-Head Attention

  • Why multiple heads instead of one attention with the full dimension?
  • What are the shapes involved in MiniLM's multi-head attention (12 heads, d=384)?
  • What do attention heads actually learn? How much should we trust interpretations?
  • What is W_O for? Couldn't you just concatenate the heads?
  • Explain MQA/GQA in terms of heads (the modern serving question).

Residuals, LayerNorm & FFN

  • Why are residual connections essential in transformers?
  • LayerNorm vs BatchNorm — why do transformers use LayerNorm?
  • What does the FFN do that attention can't, and why expand 4×?
  • Where do a transformer's parameters actually sit, proportionally?
  • A 20-layer MLP without residuals fails to train; with them it works. Explain to a junior engineer.

The Encoder Block & Stacking

  • Draw/describe a transformer encoder block from memory.
  • Why must the block preserve its input shape, and what does that enable?
  • What changes between layer 1 and layer 6 of an encoder, functionally?
  • Given a config.json, estimate the parameter count.
  • MiniLM-L6 vs MiniLM-L12: how do you choose, and what exactly differs?

Decoder, Masking & Generation

  • What is causal (masked) self-attention and why is it needed?
  • Why is decoder TRAINING parallel but INFERENCE serial?
  • Architecturally, what's the difference between BERT and GPT?
  • What are prefill and decode phases, and why do they perform so differently?
  • What is exposure bias?

Encoder vs Decoder vs Both

  • Encoder-only vs decoder-only vs encoder-decoder — when do you use each?
  • What exactly is cross-attention and where does it appear?
  • Why did decoder-only become the standard for large models?
  • Could you use a decoder-only LLM to produce embeddings? Trade-offs?
  • BERT can see both directions — why can't it generate text well?
