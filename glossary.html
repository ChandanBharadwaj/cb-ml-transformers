Glossary

Every important term from the course, A–Z, each linking back to the page that teaches it. This list grows as modules are published.

A

Attention — score a query against every key (dot products), softmax into weights, blend the values: a differentiable, learnable lookup. The core mechanism of modern AI. → Attention Is Born

Autoregressive generation — produce a token, append it, run again: one full model pass per output token. Why LLMs stream. → Decoder, Masking & Generation

B

BPE (Byte-Pair Encoding) — build a vocabulary by repeatedly merging the most frequent adjacent pair; frequent words stay whole, everything else spells out of pieces. → Tokenization

Bias — the learned vector added after a layer's matrix multiply (the +b in Wx + b); lets the layer shift space, not just warp it. → Matrices Transform Meaning

Backpropagation — blame flowing backward through the network, multiplying by each stage's local slope (chain rule), yielding every parameter's gradient in one sweep. → Backpropagation, Intuitively

Batch / mini-batch — the handful of examples processed per training step; its size trades gradient smoothness against memory. → The Training Loop

C

Causal mask — the −∞ staircase in attention scores that blocks looking at future tokens; the one difference between BERT's body and GPT's. → Decoder, Masking & Generation

Cross-attention — attention where queries come from the decoder and keys/values from the encoder's output; how translation models and Whisper consult their input. → Encoder vs Decoder vs Both

Contrastive learning — training by pulling positive pairs together and pushing negatives apart in embedding space; powers word2vec, sentence-transformers, CLIP, and RAG retrievers. → Negative Sampling

Cross-entropy — the loss −log P(truth): a surprise meter that punishes confident mistakes hardest. What LLMs train on. → Softmax & Cross-Entropy

Cosine similarity — the dot product of two vectors after removing their lengths; measures direction agreement, from −1 to 1. The score behind every vector search. → Dot Product = Similarity

D

Distributional hypothesis — "you shall know a word by the company it keeps": words in similar contexts have similar meanings; the license for learning meaning from raw text. → One-Hot & Why It Fails

Dropout — randomly zeroing activations during training so features can't co-adapt; off at inference. → Optimizers & Regularization

Dimension — one slot in a vector. all-MiniLM's embeddings have 384 of them. → Vectors Are Meaning

Dot product — multiply two vectors slot-by-slot and add it all up; one number measuring agreement. → Dot Product = Similarity

E

Encoder / decoder — the reads-bidirectionally stack (BERT/MiniLM: vectors out) vs the causally-masked stack (GPT: next tokens out); enc-dec combines both via cross-attention. → Encoder vs Decoder vs Both

Epoch — one full pass over the training set; fine-tuning uses a few, LLM pretraining often less than one. → The Training Loop

Embedding — a learned vector that represents meaning, placed so that similar things are near each other. → Vectors Are Meaning

G

Gradient (mini-batch / SGD) — real training estimates the slope on a small random batch instead of the whole dataset: noisier, vastly cheaper, and the noise even helps. → Slopes, Gradients & Learning

Gradient — the slope of the loss in every direction at once; points uphill, so training steps the other way. → Slopes, Gradients & Learning

Gradient descent — repeat: measure the slope, step downhill. The algorithm behind all model training. → Slopes, Gradients & Learning

F

FFN (feed-forward network) — the per-token expand→GELU→compress block after attention; holds ~⅔ of a transformer's parameters and most of its stored facts. → Residuals, LayerNorm & FFN

K

KV / Q (query, key, value) — a token's three learned roles: what I seek, what I advertise, what I hand over. Attention = softmax(QKᵀ/√d)·V. → Self-Attention: Q, K, V

L

LayerNorm — re-center and re-scale each token's vector (then learned γ, β); the thermostat keeping hundreds of residual additions numerically sane. → Residuals, LayerNorm & FFN

Language model — a machine assigning probabilities to the next token given a prefix; every LLM capability is this task in a loop. → Language Modeling & Perplexity

Leakage — test information contaminating training (shared preprocessing stats, duplicates across splits, temporal mixing); the silent killer of honest metrics. → The Training Loop

Logit — a raw pre-softmax score (w·x + b); one per class or vocabulary token at a model's output. → The Neuron

Learning rate — how big each downhill step is. Too big bounces, too small crawls. → Slopes, Gradients & Learning

Loss — a single number scoring how wrong the model currently is; training exists to push it down. → Slopes, Gradients & Learning

M

Multi-head attention — h parallel attentions in d/h-dim learned subspaces, concatenated and mixed by W_O; several glance patterns for the price of one. → Multi-Head Attention

Matrix — a grid of numbers that transforms vectors; every "layer" of a neural network is one (plus a bend). → Matrices Transform Meaning

N

Negative sampling — replacing the full-vocabulary softmax with "beat k plausible fakes": pull the real pair together, push sampled fakes apart. → Negative Sampling

Neuron — weighted sum + bias + a nonlinear bend; a layer is a matrix of them. → The Neuron

Norm / normalization — a vector's length (√ of summed squares); dividing by it gives a unit vector so only direction carries meaning. MiniLM normalizes its outputs. → Vectors Are Meaning

O

One-hot vector — a vocabulary-sized vector with a single 1; makes every word orthogonal to every other, so similarity is structurally impossible. → One-Hot & Why It Fails

Overfitting — memorizing training data instead of learning patterns; visible as train loss falling while validation loss rises. → The Training Loop

P

Perplexity — e^loss: the number of tokens a language model effectively hesitates between per step. Lower = better. → Language Modeling & Perplexity

Positional encoding — the order-stamp added to (or rotated into) token vectors, since attention itself is order-blind: sinusoids, learned rows (the 512 limit), or RoPE. → Embeddings & Positional Encoding

Precision / recall / F1 — "when I flag, am I right?" / "do I find them all?" / their strict harmonic mean. The measurement kit for imbalanced problems. → Measuring Models

Parameter — one learned number inside a model. "7B model" = seven billion of these. → Matrices Transform Meaning

R

Residual connection — x + layer(x): each layer adds a correction to a highway whose backward slope is exactly 1, curing vanishing gradients and making depth safe. → Residuals, LayerNorm & FFN

RoPE (rotary position embedding) — rotate Q and K by position-proportional angles so attention depends on relative token distance; the position scheme behind 128k contexts. → Embeddings & Positional Encoding

S

Self-attention — a sentence attending to itself: every token queries every token, updating each vector with weighted context. The bank-problem killer. → Self-Attention: Q, K, V

Seq2seq bottleneck — forcing a whole input through one fixed-size vector between encoder and decoder; its measured failure gave birth to attention. → Seq2Seq & the Bottleneck

Skip-gram — word2vec's flavor where the center word predicts its context words via two embedding matrices and a dot product. → word2vec: Skip-Gram Internals

Softmax — exponentiate-and-normalize: logits → probabilities; temperature is a divisor inside it. → Softmax & Cross-Entropy

Static vs contextual embeddings — one fixed vector per word (word2vec) vs a fresh vector per word per sentence (BERT/MiniLM); the "bank" problem is the divide. → Properties & Limits

Similarity matrix — every pairwise cosine at once (E·Eᵀ): diagonal of 1s, symmetric, topic clusters visible as blocks. The same grid shape underlies attention (M5) and contrastive training (M9). → Dot Product = Similarity

T

Token / tokenization — the subword units models actually read; all context limits and prices are counted in them. ~¾ of an English word each. → Tokenization

Temperature — a divisor applied to logits before softmax: low = sharp/deterministic, high = flat/creative. The API parameter, demystified. → Softmax & Cross-Entropy

V

Vanishing gradient — blame decaying to nothing through long products of small local slopes; the disease that residual connections cure. → Backpropagation, Intuitively

Vector — an ordered list of numbers; equivalently a point or an arrow in space. The universal currency of ML. → Vectors Are Meaning
